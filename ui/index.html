                  How asset packs work, and some tips
                  
    
                    
    What is a pack? A theme for your Flipper: desktop animations, icons, passport art and fonts. Only add what you want to change. Everything else keeps its normal look.
    
                    
    Making one
    
                    
      
                      
    1. Type a name and press Create pack.
      2. 
                      
    2. Add animations, passport art, icons or fonts.
      3. 
                      
    3. Press Install to Flipper.
      4. 
                      
    4. On your Flipper, open Momentum Settings > Interface > Graphics > Asset pack and pick it. Your Flipper restarts when you back out.
      5. 
                    
    
                    
    For the best look
    
                    
      
                      
    • The screen is 128x64 and only black or white. Bold shapes with strong contrast work best; photos rarely do.
      • 
                      
    • Small icons look cleanest with the None style. For bigger pictures, try Smooth.
      • 
                      
    • Transparent parts of a picture come out empty.
      • 
                      
    • Workbench resizes each icon to the size Flipper expects, so square pictures suit square icons.
      • 
                    
    
                    
    Keep it light. Your Flipper holds all of a pack's icons and fonts in memory, plus every frame of the animation that's playing. Short animations of 10 to 30 frames are plenty. If the memory bar turns red, remove frames or icons, or your Flipper may run out of memory and crash.
    
                    
    Importing. Pick a zip or folder holding a ready-made pack, a source pack with PNG files, or a dolphin folder with a manifest.txt. Workbench checks every file, leaves out anything broken and tells you what it skipped. You can then edit the pack like one you made.
    
                    
    If something looks wrong, set Asset pack back to Default on your Flipper. To replace the pack you're using, switch to Default first, then install.
    
                    
    Asset packs need Momentum firmware. On other firmware you can still build a pack and save it as a zip.
