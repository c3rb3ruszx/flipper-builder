MIFARE keysYour own keys the Flipper tries when reading MIFARE Classic cards
Keys you add here go to nfc/assets/mf_classic_dict_user.nfc; the Flipper tries them on top of its built-in list. One key is 12 hex characters, e.g. A0A1A2A3A4A5.
Wi-Fi dev boardFlash the ESP32-S2 Wi-Fi board: official firmware or your own file
1. Unplug the board from the Flipper. 2. Hold its BOOT button while plugging its own USB port into this PC, then let go. 3. Pick a firmware and flash. Only ESP32-S2 firmware is accepted, and each part is checked before anything is written.
or (or drop a .bin / .zip / .tgz here)
BluetoothExperimental: mirror and control your Flipper without a cable
Turn Bluetooth on (on the Flipper and on this PC), then scan. The first time, your Flipper shows a PIN and Windows asks for it. While mirroring over Bluetooth, the screen, buttons, screenshots and GIFs work; files and builds still use USB.
Phone remoteYour Flipper's screen and buttons on your phone, over your home Wi-Fi
Open the link (or scan the code) on a phone on the same Wi-Fi. It only works on your home network, only with this link's secret code, and stops when you switch it off or close Workbench. Windows may ask once whether to allow Workbench on private networks: allow it for this to work.

Companion appA small app on your Flipper that shows what Workbench did
Builds the Workbench companion for your Flipper's firmware and installs it to Apps > Tools. Open it on the Flipper to see when you were last connected, backed up and synced.
